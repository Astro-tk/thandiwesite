One Community. Many Pathways.

Five connected pillars of opportunity

Our programmes are not separate projects. A learner may begin with ballroom dancing, chess, music or academic support, grow in confidence, and progress towards further education, employment and a productive role in their community.

01

Pillar One

Education & Academic Excellence

Helping learners perform better and access additional educational opportunities.

Support a Learner

STEM & Academic Support

Free additional academic support for Grade 11 and Grade 12 learners at Ekuphumleni Senior Secondary, focused on Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences and other STEM subjects. Extra lessons during the week help learners build confidence and improve their chances of success.

Support a Learner
Second Chance

Matric Rewrite Programme

Supporting young people who need another opportunity to complete or improve their Grade 12 results, through academic support, study and examination preparation, mentorship, motivation and access to information about further education and employment.

Academic Support

Ongoing academic support that helps learners across grades keep pace, build study skills and reach their potential, laying the foundation for the rest of the Thandiwe pathway.

Pillar Two

Talent, Arts & Culture

Using ballroom dancing, chess, music and creative activities to discover and develop talent, confidence and discipline.

Flagship Programme

Thandiwe Ballroom Development Programme

Introducing ballroom dancing to learners at Nonyaniso Primary, St Boniface Primary and Ekuphumleni Senior Secondary, developing confidence, discipline, creativity, teamwork and self-expression. The programme culminates in the Annual Thandiwe Ballroom Dancing Competition, launching 2026.

Pillar Three

Youth Development & Employment

Preparing young people for employment, further education and adulthood.

04

Pillar Four

Health & Community Wellbeing

Empowering young people with accurate health information and support.

Know Your Status. Know Your Value.

HIV & AIDS Awareness & Youth Health

This programme is being developed to empower young people with accurate information about HIV and AIDS, sexual health, prevention, testing and stigma reduction, through awareness sessions, youth discussion groups and community events.

Testing and clinical services will be delivered through appropriately qualified healthcare partners where applicable. Thandiwe is not a medical provider.

Partner as a Health Provider

A programme in development

We are building this programme carefully and responsibly, in partnership with qualified health professionals, so that young people receive accurate information and are connected to trusted services.

Community Kitchen

A meal. A moment. A reason to hope.

The Thandiwe Community Kitchen aims to provide daily meals to vulnerable and underprivileged members of the community. Your support helps put food on the table for a family in need.

School Uniform Drive

Each year from December, Thandiwe collects school uniforms for children from vulnerable families ahead of the January school year, in partnership with Nonyaniso Primary and St Boniface Primary. December is for collection and January for distribution.
