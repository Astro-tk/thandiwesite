In Development

HIV & AIDS Awareness & Youth Health

Know Your Status. Know Your Value.

This programme is being developed to empower young people with accurate information about HIV and AIDS, sexual health, prevention, testing and stigma reduction, through awareness sessions, youth discussion groups and community events.

Testing and clinical services will be delivered through appropriately qualified healthcare partners where applicable. Thandiwe is not a medical provider.

School Uniform Drive

Give a child the confidence to start the school year

Each year from December, Thandiwe collects school uniforms for children from vulnerable families ahead of the January school year, in partnership with Nonyaniso Primary and St Boniface Primary. December is for collection and January for distribution.
