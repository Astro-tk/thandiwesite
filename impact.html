Transparency

Built to earn the trust of funders

As Thandiwe grows, this page will hold the information that institutional funders, corporates and partners rely on, published responsibly and backed by real data.

  • Registration and NPO documentation
  • Annual programme and impact reports
  • Governance and board information
  • Child safeguarding and privacy policies

We do not manufacture statistics.

Every number we publish is supported by actual programme data. Where a figure is still being recorded, we say so, because credibility matters more than an impressive headline.
