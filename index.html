Thandiwe is a community development organisation creating opportunities for children, young people and vulnerable communities through education, talent development, youth empowerment, health and social support.

Our Programmes

Five Connected Pillars of Opportunity

01

Education

Helping learners perform better and access additional educational opportunities, including STEM support, matric rewrite and academic tutoring.

02

Talent & Culture

Using ballroom dancing, chess, music and creative activities to discover and develop confidence, discipline and talent.

03

Youth Development

Preparing young people for employment, further education and adulthood through job readiness, mentorship and career guidance.

04

Community Wellbeing

Supporting vulnerable families through food security, health awareness and practical community initiatives.

05

Opportunity

Connecting young people and communities to the opportunities that can change the trajectory of their lives.

Every young person has potential.

But potential requires opportunity. Thandiwe exists to create those opportunities.
