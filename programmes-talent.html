Flagship Programme

Thandiwe Ballroom Development Programme

Introducing ballroom dancing to learners at Nonyaniso Primary, St Boniface Primary and Ekuphumleni Senior Secondary, developing confidence, discipline, creativity, teamwork and self-expression. The programme culminates in the Annual Thandiwe Ballroom Dancing Competition, launching 2026.

Middleburg Chess Club

Introducing children and young people to chess while developing critical thinking, problem solving, concentration, strategy, patience and confidence, building towards an annual Middleburg chess competition.
