Who We Are

A community development organisation rooted in Middelburg

Thandiwe is a community development organisation working primarily in Middelburg, Eastern Cape. We create opportunities for children, young people and vulnerable families to learn, discover their potential, build confidence and create better futures.

We are not a collection of separate projects. We are building a long term ecosystem of opportunity. A learner may begin with ballroom dancing, chess, music or academic support, develop confidence and skills, and progress towards further education, job readiness and a productive role in their community. This is the Thandiwe pathway.

Locally rooted in the Eastern Cape.

Connected programmes, not once-off projects.

Focused on potential, dignity and opportunity.

Our Vision

A community where every child and young person has the opportunity to learn, develop their talents, build a meaningful future and contribute to their community.

Our Mission

To create practical opportunities for children, youth and vulnerable communities through education, talent development, youth empowerment, health awareness and social support.

Our Story

Why Thandiwe exists

1
Why we began

Thandiwe was established to respond to a simple truth: there is talent in every community, but opportunity is not shared equally.

2
Why Middelburg

We began in Middelburg, Eastern Cape, working alongside local schools and families who want more for their children.

3
The challenge we address

Young people do not experience educational disadvantage, unemployment and vulnerability as separate problems. We walk alongside them through every stage of their journey.

4
The next five to ten years

Our vision is to grow into a trusted community development platform that connects learners to education, talent, health and employment opportunities.

5
Why partnerships matter

Lasting change is built by schools, businesses, funders and communities working together, not through once-off assistance.
